# Greenfield Health — Internal Overview

Greenfield Health is a fictional healthcare technology company headquartered in Seattle, Washington. The company was founded in 2019 to build secure clinical documentation tools for hospitals.

## Leadership

- **CEO:** Priya Nair
- **CTO:** Marcus Chen
- **Chief Medical Officer:** Dr. Amelia Brooks

## Products

- **CareChart** — electronic clinical notes platform launched in 2021
- **MedSync** — patient scheduling and referral workflow tool launched in 2023

## Compliance

Greenfield Health stores all production patient data in encrypted databases. The company is preparing for HIPAA-aligned audits in 2026.

## Partnerships

Greenfield Health partners with **Summit Cloud** for managed hosting in the US-West region.
